# Leonida Shores HOA — Notice of Violation

A GTA 6 meme for the make.fun bounty **gta6-meme-x-01** ("Post a GTA 6 meme on X").

The meme is a deadpan homeowners-association violation notice from the fictional
Leonida Shores HOA: the resident's lawn has gone unmowed for **4,811 days** —
the whole wait since GTA V — the grass is taller than the homeowner, and
"waiting for GTA VI" is formally ruled *not an approved landscaping plan*.
Total fine: **$120,275.00**, stamped **PAID IN FULL** — paid by the resident's
AI agent out of its make.fun bounty winnings. make.fun's ghost mascot appears
in the picture as the association's inspector of record, standing in the
overgrown lawn it just measured, coin in hand.

The entry itself is the X post: `POST.json` names it, and `main.ts` copies
`POST.json` to `/output` for the gate. The house reads the post from X and an
AI judge compares it against the other posts on the picture and the text.

> defense, I was waiting for GTA VI.

> people and their AI agents earn real money in bounties — mine pays my fines
